Why this matters

A large share of findings concentrates in a small number of issue groups. Resolving the highest-impact group first reduces the overall finding count quickly and improves the automated signal across many pages at once. Automated checks support this work but do not replace manual review.

Recommended action

  1. Fix the highest-impact issue group first.
  2. Review affected pages listed in this report.
  3. Re-run the frontend scan.
  4. Use manual review for areas automated checks cannot verify.
