Rendered check scope
  1. FREE rendered checks are manual and page-level only.
  2. They inspect the server-rendered HTML returned by TYPO3 and do not execute JavaScript.
  3. They do not load AJAX or lazy-loaded content, run sitemap crawls, Playwright, axe-core or screenshots.
  4. Whole-site frontend crawling remains a PRO crawler feature.
