About custom rule lists
  1. Custom phrases extend the built-in defaults — they never replace them.
  2. Phrases are matched case-insensitively and trimmed of whitespace.
  3. For phrase rules, the match is on the link's accessible name (label + aria-label).
  4. You do not need to clear TYPO3 caches — new rules apply to the next scan.
